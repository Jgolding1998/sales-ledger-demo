Filters

Date basis decides which date must fall between From and To (both inclusive): the invoice date or the order date.

As-of date controls invoice visibility and balances: invoices dated after it are excluded (invoice basis) or carry no balance (order basis), and a payment only reduces the balance when its payment date is on or before the as-of date. Overdue means past due date as of that day.
